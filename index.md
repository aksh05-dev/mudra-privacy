# Mudra privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Mudra is a digital philately passport. You open the app in a district of India and it adds that district's stamp to your passport.

## What Mudra collects
**Nothing.** Mudra has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The app does not send any information off your phone.

## Location
When you open the app, Mudra asks your phone for your location **once**, to work out which district you are in. Mudra:
- reads the location only while the app is open, never in the background;
- compares it with district boundaries stored inside the app, on your phone, without using the internet;
- keeps **only the district and the date** of each stamp. Your exact location is discarded straight away and never saved or shared.

You can turn location access off at any time in your phone's settings. You can still browse your passport without it.

## What is stored on your phone
Your passport lives only on your phone: the name you typed, your stamps (district and date), any notes you write, and app settings. If you delete the app, this data is deleted.

## Backups and sharing
Mudra can create a backup file (`.mudra`) or a picture of a stamp **only when you ask it to**. It hands the file to your phone's share sheet, and you choose where it goes, such as your own Google Drive or a chat. Mudra itself uploads nothing.

## Children
Mudra does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
